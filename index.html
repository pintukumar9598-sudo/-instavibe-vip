GOOD TO KNOW

A little clarity.

Will these bios appear again?

Fresh finds and Quick Bio remember displayed entries on this browser. When you've seen the full set, you choose when to restart. Search and mood collections let you revisit any entry. Clearing browser data also clears this history.

Are the names available on Instagram or in my game?

Availability isn't checked. The handle generator creates simple handle candidates; decorated names are intended for display names or games that support those symbols. Check the destination app before using a paid rename card.

Is this a live list of viral trends?

No. The October edit contains newly written, style-inspired text. Minimal, playful Hinglish, poetcore, cool-blue and celestial directions informed the edit. No bio or hashtag can guarantee reach. You can read the inspiration notes in About & settings.

Why won't the welcome animation play again?

It's reserved for a first visit. Refreshing, changing pages and clearing your fresh-bio history won't replay it. A new browser or cleared site data can look like a new visitor. If durable storage is blocked, the intro is skipped.

What stays on my device?

Local tools, drafts and saved items work in your browser. AI chat sends the message and recent conversation to the connected AI service only when you send or use a shortcut. Optional usage analytics is off by default.
